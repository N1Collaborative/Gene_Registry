# N1C Gene Registry

The **N1C Gene Registry** is a publicly accessible resource developed by the **N=1 Collaborative** to support the identification and development of genetic therapies for rare diseases.

🌐 **Access the live registry:**
https://generegistry.n1collaborative.org/

## About the Registry

The registry brings together information relevant to genetic therapeutic development, including:

* **N-of-1 Projects** – ongoing and available N-of-1 and N-of-few therapeutic development projects
* **Marketed Genetic Therapies** – approved and marketed genetic therapies
* **Assessed Variants** – curated assessments of variants and their therapeutic eligibility
* **Published Genetic Therapies** – genetic therapies reported in the scientific literature
* **Assessed Genes & Diseases** – gene- and disease-level assessments of potential genetic therapeutic strategies
* **Automated Assessment Tools** – tools supporting automated therapeutic eligibility assessment

The registry is continuously updated and expanded.

## Contributing

Researchers and developers working on individualized genetic therapies are encouraged to contribute their projects to the registry. Further information and submission options are available through the live website.

## N=1 Collaborative

The N1C Gene Registry is an initiative of the **N=1 Collaborative (N1C)**, supporting collaboration and knowledge sharing in individualized genetic medicine.
